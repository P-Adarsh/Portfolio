About Me

I'm a final-year Computer Science Engineering student with a genuine curiosity for how data becomes insight, and how insight becomes something useful. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, data analytics and practical software engineering — I like problems that start messy and end explainable.

Across my projects I've handled the full pipeline: cleaning and exploring data, engineering features, training and evaluating models, and packaging results into something a non-technical reader can act on. I care about rigor as much as results — confusion matrices and cross-validation aren't afterthoughts, they're how I know a model earned its conclusions.

Outside of models and notebooks, I'm drawn to automation and the small tools that quietly save people time. I'm currently looking for a role where I can apply that same analytical, problem-first mindset to real data-driven software.
